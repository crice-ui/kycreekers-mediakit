01 — About

We're Matt and Chris. We uncover history in Kentucky's creeks.

KyCreekers is one of the fastest-growing bottle-digging and history-hunting communities online. Every weekend, we wade through creeks, dig through century-old dumps and privies, and pull artifacts from the ground — 100-year-old bottles, stamped bricks, relics, arrowheads, and pieces of forgotten American history. We go live. We document everything. And we tell the stories behind what we find.

Matt Franklin

The face of KyCreekers. On-camera host, bottle hunter, and antique collector. Started creek walking as a teenager with his grandparents and cousins. Built KyCreekers on TikTok during COVID and hasn't stopped since.

Chris "Pookie"

Co-creator and the engine behind the scenes. Research, production, and strategy. Out in the creeks every single weekend alongside Matt, building the channel from the ground up.
